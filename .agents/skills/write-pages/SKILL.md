# Write the page

Notes and chat are the record of what is true. The page is a piece of writing. Read the source, decide how a reader should meet it, and write that. Do not paste the source in.

## What stays

Keep every fact, name, number, caveat, and degree of certainty. If the notes say "probably," the page says probably. If a step, tool, or detail only fits one device, say who it is for.

Do not add steps, causes, results, advice, or emphasis the source did not give. If the page needs something that was never said, leave it out or ask.

Product names, UI labels, commands, and certificate or profile names stay exact.

## What changes

Wording, order inside the page, and paragraph shape are yours. Spoken drafts repeat, circle, and stall. The page says each thing once, in the place a reader needs it, and the next sentence should feel like the reason to keep reading.

Before you write, read the page you are editing and the section around the new text. Join what is already there. Do not drop in a block that restates it.

Write in a calm, direct, non-judgmental voice. Short sentences and longer ones both belong. Cut filler that is only the draft thinking out loud ("that's huge," "you have to really"). Keep emphasis when the source is actually stressing a point.

For a procedure, a reader should be able to do the steps in order. Still write them. A transcript of the explanation is not a guide.

## Example

Source:

> Music is a really, really big trigger. It's probably the main trigger for everyone. Music is so tempting because it can make a daydream incredibly good — so much more fun if there's music playing. You have to get rid of your music first. You have to really manage it and hone it. That's huge.

Page:

> Music is probably the main trigger. A daydream is much more fun with music playing, which is why it is so hard to leave alone. Deal with music first, and keep managing it after that. That matters more than the other blocks.

Same claims: music is probably the main trigger, it makes the daydream better, it comes first, and it stays something you manage. The draft's repetitions are gone.
